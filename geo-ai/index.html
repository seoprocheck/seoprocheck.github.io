Check how AI search sees your site

Getting cited by AI search starts with two basics: letting the right crawlers in, and writing pages that say clearly what they are about. The tools on this page cover both. Some look at access, through your robots.txt and llms.txt files. Others look at clarity, by checking whether your text names its subject early, defines it and backs it up.

Which tool for which job

  • AI Crawler Checker: see which of 27 AI crawlers, such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, your robots.txt allows or blocks, and which ones it never names.
  • LLMs.txt Generator: draft an llms.txt file from your site name, a short description, your key topics and your most important pages.
  • Entity Clarity Checker: paste a page and name its main subject to check that you mention it early, define it in a plain sentence and support it with numbers.
  • AI Content Readiness Checker: paste the HTML or text of a page to check for structured data, headings, lists, sources and definitions.
  • Citation Formatter: turn a source and its key finding into an inline citation, a reference line and an HTML snippet.

An Allowed result means your robots.txt permits that bot. A firewall or CDN rule can still turn it away, so confirm real visits in your server logs. The two content checkers score simple patterns, such as definition phrases, numbers and word counts, so read their scores as a list of things to review, not as a grade. Pasting robots.txt into the AI Crawler Checker keeps everything in your browser; the optional live fetch sends the domain you type to a public proxy.

To check whether a firewall is blocking crawlers your robots.txt allows, read Is Your Cloudflare or WAF Secretly Blocking GPTBot?
