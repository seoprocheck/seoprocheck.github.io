Check what a page actually says

These six tools look at the words on a page rather than the code around it: how long it is, how it is structured, how easy it is to read, how often it repeats a keyword and which common questions it leaves out. You paste your text or HTML and get the result right away, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Which tool for which job

  • SERP Preview Tool: see how your title and meta description may look in Google results, with character counts as you type.
  • Word Counter: count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, with reading and speaking time.
  • Keyword Density Checker: count how often a keyword or phrase appears and flag heavy repetition.
  • Heading Structure Analyzer: see your H1 to H6 headings as an outline, with a missing or extra H1 and skipped levels flagged.
  • Reading Level Checker: get a Flesch-Kincaid grade level and a Flesch Reading Ease score.
  • Content Gap Finder: check a draft against common angles for a topic, such as what is, how to, versus and mistakes, and get questions you could add.

The Heading Structure Analyzer needs HTML, so paste the page source rather than the visible text. Treat density and reading scores as prompts to reread a passage, not as targets to hit. The Content Gap Finder works from a fixed list of question patterns, not from competitor pages, so use it for ideas, then look at the pages that rank for your topic before you add new sections.
